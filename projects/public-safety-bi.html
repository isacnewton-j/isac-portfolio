Police RMS & EZ Fire Records

Public Safety BI Platform

Centralized BI platform for county-level Police and Fire agencies across the United States, covering high-volume incident data and regulatory submissions (NFIRS), integrated with NCIC.

Power BI SQL Server SSIS SSAS ER Studio Windows Server

Problem

Multiple systems and jurisdictions needed a single source of truth for incident analytics, operational reporting, and forecasting, while keeping access secure and performance predictable.

My Approach

Designed data marts and a warehouse, built incremental SSIS pipelines, shipped SSAS cubes with partitions and role-based security, and delivered Power BI reports aligned to how officers and leadership work.
